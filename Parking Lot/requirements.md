# Parking Lot

## Requirements

1. The application will provide support for multistory parking.
2. User will be provided with ticket at entry time.
3. The bill will be generated at time of exit.
4. The system will allocate the parking spot at time of entry.
5. User can pay from multiple payment methods.

## Entities

1. Parking Strategy
2. Bill
3. Ticket
4. Vehicle
5. Vehicle Type
6. Parking Manager
7. Parking
8. Floor
9. Slot
10. Fee Calculation Strategy
